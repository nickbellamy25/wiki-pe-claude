This is a practical, self-paced guide to using **Claude.ai** in your day-to-day work. It's written for [Team Name] professionals, not engineers.

By the end, you'll be able to brief Claude properly, organize your work into Projects, and run the everyday deal and research tasks with confidence and the right level of rigor.

## What this track covers

- **Foundations**: why context is everything, how to write a good prompt, how to turn it into a reusable template, and how to set standing instructions.
- **Projects**: structuring your work into reusable function projects and siloed deal projects.
- **Power features**: connectors, the analysis tool, and how to unlock extra value out of Claude.
- **Best practices**: verification discipline, what not to do, and a one-page cheat sheet.

## How to navigate

Use the left-hand menu to move between sections. If you're starting cold, work top to bottom. If you have a specific task in mind, jump straight to the relevant section.
